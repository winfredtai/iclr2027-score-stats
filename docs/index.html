Notes

  • Rating scale. ICLR 2027 uses a 4-point overall rating with no borderline option: 1 = clear reject, 2 = leaning reject, 3 = leaning accept, 4 = clear accept.
  • Paper average. The unweighted mean of a paper's current overall ratings. Confidence is not used for weighting. About 90% of papers have exactly 3 reviews, so most averages fall on thirds (2.00, 2.33, 2.67, …). The histogram intervals start on those values.
  • Which papers count. Active submissions with at least one public official review. Withdrawn and desk-rejected papers are excluded. Papers that withdraw after seeing their reviews tend to score low, so averages may drift upward over time as they leave the pool.
  • High-scoring papers. Each refresh saves the list of active submissions with an average of 3.00 or higher, with titles linked to OpenReview. New means the paper was below 3.00 at the previous refresh. ▲ / ▼ means its average changed since then. Papers that drop below 3.00 or are withdrawn leave the list.
  • Areas. The primary area the authors picked at submission (21 areas).
  • ICLR 2026 accept rate. Accepted ÷ (accepted + rejected + withdrawn) for the same primary area at ICLR 2026, from OpenReview decisions, with desk-rejected papers excluded. That came to 28% overall; counting only papers that received a decision, it was 39%. It is for reference only. Some papers in the current pool will still withdraw, so the eventual acceptance rate among today's active papers will fall between these two figures.
  • Scores are not final. Reviewers can raise or lower their ratings during the discussion period, which ends Nov 18, 2026, and the AC discussion that follows. Final decisions are due Dec 16, 2026.
